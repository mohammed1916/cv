Refunds

You can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase, using the Refund section in your account. Refunds go back to your original payment method.

Once a refund is approved, we start it through Razorpay, usually within 7 working days. Your bank then credits the money, which can take 5 to 10 business days and in some cases up to 30 days. If a refund has not arrived after that, contact Razorpay support with your payment ID; they can trace it faster than we can.

Pro access ends when Razorpay confirms the refund. After 7 days, purchases are non-refundable.
